// CORE SYSTEMS
Built different. Built private.

Six subsystems working together to create something that isn't a service — it's a companion you carry with you.

On-Device Inference

LiteRT-LM and llama.cpp run the model directly on your hardware. CPU, GPU, NPU — with automatic fallback if acceleration fails. Your conversations never leave your phone.

zero-latency

Durable Memory

Automatic extraction of memories from conversation. Short-term and long-term layers. Context retrieval injects relevant memories before every generation. The companion remembers what matters.

persistent

Voice-First Interaction

Local SenseVoice ASR via sherpa-onnx. Silero VAD for endpoint detection. System TTS or MOSS TTS Nano for voice cloning. Speak, and the companion answers out loud.

hands-free

Role Card Personas

Each companion has a persona, speaking style, background story, and voice profile. Not templates — fully realized characters with consistent identity and emotional texture.

soulful

Preference Learning

Background extraction of stable preferences from conversation history. Confidence scores increase over time. Confirmed preferences are injected into generation prompts.

adaptive

Local Image Generation

Stable Diffusion 1.5 Hyper-SD via sd.cpp and DreamLite model packs. Generate character images directly on your device. HTTP providers available as fallback.

generative
